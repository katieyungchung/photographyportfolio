Hi, I'm Katie!

I'm a photographer based in the Bay Area. I've always had a passion for visual arts and taking pictures, and I took my first photography class in 8th grade. Throughout high school, I was involved in ASB Tech and Film Club, where I got a lot of experience in working with a camera. I used to focus more on videography/film, but these days I've been trying to get more experience with photography. This portfolio is a compilation of my work so far, so I hope you enjoy and consider doing a shoot with me sometime!

My Process

The camera that I use for photography is the Sony a6000. It was given to me by my brother-in-law, and ever since I've cherished it as a tool for my creativity and expression. I love to take pictures that incorporate nature and warm golden hour/morning lighting. I often like the soft glow that comes with back lighting, so I often position my subjects in front of the sun to create a "halo effect". I also like to choose locations that really highlight nature. I'll list some of my favorite locations to take pictures at below.

Goals And Aspirations

My goal is to delve into photography for larger scale events like concerts, weddings, etc.
